### 🧠 ScaleBits

A digital development system for manufacturing companies that combines microlearning, knowledge assessment, employee ideas and implementation support.

The system is designed to make continuous development measurable and closely connected with real manufacturing challenges.

---

### 🤖 GuidedRobotics

A decision-support system for identifying and prioritizing robotics opportunities in manufacturing.

GuidedRobotics analyzes production processes, operations, work-in-progress, losses and constraints to help determine:

- where robotization is technically feasible;
- where automation can create the highest operational value;
- which process losses can be reduced;
- which robotics projects should be prioritized.

The goal is to move robotics investment decisions from intuition toward structured operational and economic analysis.

The system connects:

**process analysis — production losses — robotization potential — economic effect — project priority**

## Personal projects

### 🏭 Data-Driven Operational Efficiency

An interactive book and engineering toolkit for manufacturing leaders.

The project explores how operational data can be transformed into:

The interactive edition combines traditional book chapters with:

---

### 📡 RoboticsPulse

A robotics intelligence system for monitoring developments across the global robotics market.

The system collects and structures information from multiple international sources and transforms a rapidly growing stream of robotics news into industrial intelligence.

It is designed to:

- identify significant robotics events;
- track companies, technologies and products;
- extract structured facts and measurable indicators;
- group duplicate publications into unique events;
- analyze market signals and trends;
- produce fact-based briefings and reports.

The project explores how AI can be used not simply to summarize information, but to build a continuously updated intelligence layer for an industrial technology market.

---

### 📊 PredictEconomy

An experimental system for monitoring economic conditions through composite indicators and scenario analysis.

The project explores how multiple heterogeneous signals can be transformed into transparent indices that support interpretation and decision-making.

The key principle is explainability.

Instead of producing a single number without context, the system is intended to connect:

**source data — indicators — composite index — changes — scenarios — interpretation**

PredictEconomy is also an experiment in building lightweight AI-assisted analytical systems that can update continuously and communicate complex dynamics in a compact form.

# How I think about industrial digitalization

For me, digital transformation is not primarily about software.

It is about creating a management system that connects the physical production environment with data, interpretation and action.

```text
Physical process
      ↓
Data acquisition
      ↓
Operational events
      ↓
Indicators
      ↓
Analytics / AI
      ↓
Management decision
      ↓
Action
      ↓
Business effect
```

The value of technology appears only when this chain changes how the company actually operates.

---

# From data to decisions

A dashboard is not the final product of a digital system.

A useful operational system should help answer:

1. **What changed?**
2. **Where did it happen?**
3. **Which orders, products, machines or processes are affected?**
4. **What are the likely explanations?**
5. **What management action should follow?**
6. **Who should act?**
7. **When should the result be checked again?**
8. **What business effect should we expect?**

This logic is central to many of the products and research projects I work on.
